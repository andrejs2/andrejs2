### Hi, I'm Andrej Seršen 👋

Lawyer working on natural language processing for legislation. I spent two decades reviewing draft
Slovenian laws and regulations for constitutionality, consistency and drafting quality; now I study
how language models read them, and where they get them wrong.

- 🔬 Research assistant, [Department of Knowledge Technologies (E8)](https://kt.ijs.si/), Jožef Stefan Institute
- 🎓 PhD student in Information and Communication Technologies, Jožef Stefan International Postgraduate School
- ⚖️ Reviewing draft laws and regulations at the Government Office for Legislation, Republic of Slovenia

#### Research interests

- Retrieval and question answering over statutory text
- Verifiability: checking every generated claim against the provision it cites
- Natural language inference for legal claims
- Knowledge graphs of legislation: references, amendments, versions in time
- Complexity and readability of regulations
- Slovenian language models (GaMS)

#### Current work

**AI Legal Assistant**: a retrieval and verification system over Slovenian law, built so that every
answer can be traced back to its source. Notes on evaluation and results are on my
[blog](https://sersen.eu/).

#### Open source

Outside research I maintain Home Assistant integrations for Slovenian public data, most notably
[ARSO weather](https://github.com/andrejs2/slovenian_weather_integration), and contribute
Slovenian voice intents to [OHF-Voice/intents](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/intents).
